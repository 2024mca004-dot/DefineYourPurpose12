Design Guidelines: Prashun Shetty Personal Brand & EdTech/SaaS Directory
Design Approach
Reference-Based: Drawing from modern SaaS platforms (Linear's clarity, Stripe's professionalism, Notion's clean layouts) combined with professional consultancy aesthetics to establish trust and authority.

Core Design Elements
Typography System
Primary Font: Inter (for clean, professional readability)

Hero Headline: 600 weight, 48px (desktop) / 32px (mobile)
Section Headers: 600 weight, 36px (desktop) / 24px (mobile)
Subheadings: 500 weight, 24px (desktop) / 18px (mobile)
Body Text: 400 weight, 16px, line-height 1.6
UI Elements: 500 weight, 14px
Captions/Tags: 400 weight, 12px
Hierarchy: Use navy blue for headers, charcoal gray (#2D3748) for body text, and gold sparingly for CTAs and accent elements.

Layout System
Spacing Units: Tailwind units of 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 (e.g., p-4, mb-8, gap-6)

Section Padding: py-20 (desktop), py-12 (mobile)
Component Spacing: mb-16 between major sections, mb-8 between related elements
Card Padding: p-6 for standard cards, p-8 for featured cards
Max Container Width: max-w-7xl (1200px) with px-6 horizontal padding
Grid System:

Companies Grid: grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3, gap-6
Company Profile: 3-column layout (lg:grid-cols-3) for contact/form sections
Blog Grid: grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3 for article cards
About Timeline: Single column on mobile, 2-column alternating on desktop
Component Library
Navigation:

Sticky header with navy blue background, white text
Logo left, navigation center, "List Your Company" gold button right
Mobile: Hamburger menu with slide-in panel
Secondary navigation for filtering (categories, price tiers, location)
Buttons:

Primary CTA: Gold background (#F1C40F), navy text, rounded-lg, px-6 py-3, 600 weight
Secondary: Navy outline, white background, navy text
Tertiary: Text-only navy, underline on hover
Glass-morphism for buttons on hero images: backdrop-blur-md bg-white/20 with white text
Cards:

Company Cards: White background, border border-gray-200, rounded-lg shadow-sm, hover:shadow-md transition
Structure: Logo at top (h-16 object-contain), title (600 weight), 2-line description (text-gray-600), tag badges (gold background, navy text, rounded-full px-3 py-1), action buttons at bottom
Featured Cards: Gold border-l-4 accent
Forms:

Input Fields: border border-gray-300, rounded-lg, px-4 py-3, focus:border-navy focus:ring-2 focus:ring-navy/20
Labels: 500 weight, mb-2, text-sm
Validation: Red text for errors, green checkmark for success
Lead Form (Company Profile): 3-column grid on desktop, stacked mobile, navy submit button
Modals:

Payment/Subscription: Centered overlay, max-w-2xl, white background, shadow-2xl
Plan Cards: Grid layout, featured plan with gold border and "Most Popular" badge
Razorpay/Stripe logos at bottom for trust
Dashboard:

Sidebar Navigation: Navy background, white text/icons, active state with gold accent
Content Area: White background, card-based layout for stats, tables, approvals
Tables: Striped rows, hover states, action buttons per row
Blog/Thought Hub:

Featured Article: Large hero with background image overlay, white text, gradient from transparent to navy
Article Cards: Thumbnail image top, category badge (gold), title, excerpt, "Read More" link
Category Pills: Horizontal scroll on mobile, gold background for active
Timeline (About Page):

Vertical line with gold accent, alternating left/right cards on desktop
Year markers in gold circles, achievement cards with white background, shadow-sm
Badges:

Speaker/Mentor: Rounded-full, gold background, navy text, icon + label
Subscription Status: Small badge on company profiles (gold for premium)
Images
Hero Section: Large background image or video thumbnail (16:9 aspect ratio) showcasing professional setting or technology. Overlay with gradient (from navy/80 to transparent) for text readability. Place headline and CTAs over image with glass-morphism buttons.

Company Logos: Contained within h-16 w-full object-contain spaces, grayscale by default, full color on hover.

About Section: Professional headshot (rounded-lg, max-w-md), speaking/mentoring photos in timeline cards.

Blog Thumbnails: 16:9 aspect ratio, object-cover, rounded-t-lg for card tops.

Accessibility
Maintain 4.5:1 contrast: Navy (#0A2342) on white, white on navy, gold (#F1C40F) only on navy backgrounds
Focus states: 2px ring-offset with navy ring color
All images include alt text placeholders
Form labels explicitly associated with inputs
Keyboard navigation: tab order follows visual flow, skip links for main content
Animations
Minimal, purposeful only:

Card hover: Subtle lift with shadow transition (duration-200)
Button hover: Slight scale (scale-105) and shadow
Page transitions: Fade-in for content sections on scroll (opacity + translate-y)
NO complex scroll-driven animations or parallax effects
Responsive Breakpoints
Mobile: base (< 768px) - Single column, stacked layouts
Tablet: md (768px - 1024px) - 2-column grids
Desktop: lg (1024px+) - 3-column grids, full navigation
This design system balances professional authority with modern web aesthetics, ensuring trust while maintaining clean, accessible user experiences across all device types.
